Biography

Lord (JD) Waverley

An international trade and investment advisor working at the intersection of business diplomacy and public policy, connecting commercial opportunity with trusted local partnerships in emerging and frontier markets.

Drawing on extensive parliamentary experience in international affairs, his engagement spans market entry and foreign direct investment, free trade agreements, trade compliance, geopolitical risk and sustainable development.
